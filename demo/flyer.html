MASA/TQC Pathway Certificate

Awarded by the Missouri Association of School Administrators (MASA) and The Quality Coach (TQC) for completion of the New/Early Career Superintendent Pathway (Onboarding, Year 1, Year 2, and Year 3).

This certificate recognizes completion of a professional learning pathway. It is not a Missouri educator certificate, license, or endorsement, and it is not issued, approved, or endorsed by the Missouri Department of Elementary and Secondary Education (DESE). Pathway content is aligned to the Missouri DESE Standards and Competencies (2022) and cross-referenced to the AASA Professional Standards for the Superintendency.

What the platform does

For superintendents

  • One pathway, one place. Phases, required events, strand or academy choices, and hours toward each phase badge, from the point of hire through Year 3.
  • Sessions that count. Join each SuperSprint and Leadership 90 from the pathway, mark it complete with a short reflection, and watch it count toward your phase badge once it is confirmed.
  • A page for every class. Each session opens its own page: when it meets and how to join, materials, and an assignment to turn in with feedback from your coach. A Coming up list keeps the next sessions and due work in view.
  • Enroll yourself. Browse the course catalog to sign up for workshops, conferences, and other open offerings, or choose your strand or academy. Everything you enroll in lands on your Coming up list.
  • Explore by role. Try a view-only sample as a mentor/coach, board president, superintendent, or site administrator. Direct tabs open phases, badges, hours, evaluation, and standards alignment. The board preview is sample-only; boards have no live login.
  • Help built in. A how-to guide for every role, with pictures, direct Show me links, and dedicated videos for the pathway tabs and demo role previews. The guide and printable PDF are open to anyone, including on the sign-in page.
  • Your information, your choice. Choose, person by person, what your coach, mentor, MASA staff, and board president can see, and write your evaluation goals by standard. A board president sees only your completed learning and the goals you share.
  • Hours and substitutions. Log coaching, mentoring, conference, and regional meeting hours, and ask to count equivalent learning in place of an event.
  • Ready for the board. Evaluation choices update alignment labels immediately, with visible save progress. A Standards Alignment Report organized by the DESE standards and tied to the board’s evaluation (MSBA, MARE, or the district’s own).
  • A printable certificate. Completing all four phases awards the MASA/TQC Pathway Certificate, ready to print or save as a PDF.

For coaches, mentors, and MASA

  • Roster at a glance. Everyone by phase, hours toward each badge, required events, choices still needed, and badges earned.
  • Approvals. Completed sessions, logged hours, and substitution requests wait for a program administrator’s decision and count toward badges as soon as they are approved. Coaches see their people’s requests.
  • Each role sees only its own people. Coaches and mentors see the superintendents assigned to them. MASA staff see the program roster, read-only.
  • Attendance and credit. Record attendance for a whole session at once, and credit academies, conferences, and in-person events in one click.
  • Accounts in one place. Program staff add people, send sign-in links, and turn accounts off from the People view. Everyone on the roster can set their own password.

Privacy and trust

  • Sign in with your email and a password you choose from a one-time emailed link. Passwords are stored only as salted, scrambled hashes, never as text, and repeated wrong guesses are locked out.
  • Coaching stays separate from evaluation: notes stay with the superintendent, their coach, and program staff. Mentors never see the evaluation. Each superintendent chooses what their coach, mentor, MASA staff, and board president can see; a board president sees only completed learning and the goals the superintendent shares.
  • Every sign-in and change is recorded.
  • No student information: the platform asks people not to enter or upload it.
