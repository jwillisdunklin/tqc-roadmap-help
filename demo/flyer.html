Phase 1Point of hire (before July 1) through the summer welcome reception

Onboarding

ALEs 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, and 8 (board relations, the DESE portal, and listening tours), the DISC and EQ assessment, and the in-person Leveraging Your Behavioral Style debrief (ALE 19, an L90+ of 2.5 hours) on the summer welcome reception day: 8.5 hours. School Finance 101 is part of onboarding until its timing is set.

Onboarding Completion Badge: 10 required events

Year 1

The New Superintendent Finance Academy (27.5 hours), ALEs 6, 7, 13, and 15, a peer superintendent mentor (10 hours), and a coach (15 to 20 hours) who may stay with you through Year 3. Every year: 4 or more regional meetings and at least one conference.

Year 1 Completion Badge: 9 required events

Year 2

Choose your path for Years 2 and 3: the Core Learning Path (ALEs 9, 10, 11, 12, 14, 16, 17, and 18 plus standalone sessions to match the Academy’s 33 hours) or the Superintendent Leadership Academy, which builds the ALE content in. Coaching continues at 15 hours, with regional meetings and a conference.

Year 3

Finish the path you chose in Year 2. Coaching continues at 15 hours, with regional meetings and a conference. Finishing Year 3 earns the Year 3 Completion Badge and the MASA/TQC Pathway Certificate of Completion.

MASA/TQC Pathway Certificate of Completion

Awarded by the Missouri Association of School Administrators (MASA) and The Quality Coach (TQC) for completing the series of trainings in the New/Early Career Superintendent Pathway, with a completion badge earned for each year: Onboarding, Year 1, Year 2, and Year 3.

This certificate of completion recognizes completion of a series of professional learning trainings. It is not a Missouri educator certificate, license, or endorsement, and it is not issued, approved, or endorsed by the Missouri Department of Elementary and Secondary Education (DESE). Pathway content is aligned to the Missouri DESE Standards and Competencies (2022) and cross-referenced to the AASA Professional Standards for the Superintendency.

What the platform does

For superintendents

  • One pathway, one place. The 19 Authentic Learning Experiences (ALEs), each tied to the DESE Superintendent Performance Indicators, plus required events, yearly regional meetings and a conference, the Years 2 and 3 path, and hours toward each phase badge, from the point of hire through Year 3.
  • Sessions that count. Join each SuperSprint, Leadership 90, and L90+ from the pathway, mark it complete with a short reflection, and watch it count toward your phase badge once it is confirmed.
  • Courses that work like courses. Each course has a Home page, modules by week or topic, assignments, quizzes, discussions, resources, and announcements. Progress bars show what is left, and finishing every requirement sends the course for pathway credit. A Coming up list keeps sessions and due work in view.
  • Enroll yourself. Browse the course catalog to sign up for standalone sessions and other open offerings, and choose your Years 2 and 3 path: the core ALEs plus standalone sessions, or the Superintendent Leadership Academy. Everything you enroll in lands on your Coming up list.
  • A public catalog and PD calendar. Every session with its date, time, and whether it is virtual or in person, plus the Academy, published well ahead so superintendents can plan. MASA can apply its own logo and colors.
  • Explore by role. Try a view-only sample as a mentor/coach, board president, superintendent, or site administrator. Direct tabs open phases, badges, hours, evaluation, and standards alignment. The board preview is sample-only; boards have no live login.
  • Help built in. A how-to guide for every role, with pictures, direct Show me links, and dedicated videos for the pathway tabs and demo role previews. The guide and printable PDF are open to anyone, including on the sign-in page.
  • Your information, your choice. Choose, person by person, what your coach, mentor, MASA staff, and board president can see, and write your evaluation goals by standard. A board president sees only your completed learning and the goals you share.
  • Course building for instructors. Administrators create courses and assign instructors (coaches and mentors can teach too). Instructors build their own courses with a visual editor, embedded videos and slide shows, documents, drafts and publishing, availability dates, prerequisites, a learner preview, rubrics, reusable quiz questions, templates, and one review page for all turned-in work.
  • Bring existing courses in. Administrators can import courses from Canvas (.imscc exports), Google Classroom (Google Takeout), and TalentLMS (course lists plus downloaded files) as unpublished drafts, with a report of anything that must be rebuilt. No student records are copied.
  • Hours and substitutions. Log coaching, mentoring, conference, and regional meeting hours (tagged with the ALEs a coaching or mentoring session connected to), and ask to count equivalent learning in place of an event.
  • Ready for the board. Evaluation choices update alignment labels immediately, with visible save progress. A Standards Alignment Report organized by the DESE standards and tied to the board’s evaluation (MSBA, MCE Policy 1720, or the district’s own).
  • A printable certificate. Each year you finish earns a completion badge, and completing the full series of trainings (all four) awards the MASA/TQC Pathway Certificate of Completion, ready to print or save as a PDF. Each earned badge has a printable proof-of-growth page to share with the board president.
  • Recognition you choose. If you turn it on, your board president, and anyone else you name, gets an email when you complete the pathway. Recognition at a MASA conference is noted on your certificate badge.

For coaches, mentors, and MASA

  • Roster at a glance. Everyone by phase, hours toward each badge, required events, choices still needed, and badges earned.
  • Approvals. Completed sessions, logged hours, and substitution requests wait for a program administrator’s decision and count toward badges as soon as they are approved. Coaches see their people’s requests.
  • Each role sees only its own people. Coaches and mentors see the superintendents assigned to them. MASA staff see the program roster, read-only.
  • Attendance and credit. Record attendance for a whole session at once, and credit academies, conferences, and in-person events in one click.
  • Accounts in one place. Program staff add people, send sign-in links, and turn accounts off from the People view, or add a whole cohort at once from a spreadsheet. Everyone on the roster can set their own password.
  • Coaching tied to the ALEs. Coaches and mentors see each superintendent’s next ALEs and record which ALEs their sessions connected to.
  • Ready for the pilot review. An engagement report (sign-ins, sessions, course activity; counts only) and participant feedback show what to change after the pilot. Program staff see every DESE performance indicator’s coverage and gaps.

Privacy and trust

  • Sign in with your email and a password you choose from a one-time emailed link. Passwords are stored only as salted, scrambled hashes, never as text, and repeated wrong guesses are locked out.
  • Coaching stays separate from evaluation: notes stay with the superintendent, their coach, and program staff. Mentors never see the evaluation. Each superintendent chooses what their coach, mentor, MASA staff, and board president can see; a board president sees only completed learning and the goals the superintendent shares.
  • Every sign-in and change is recorded.
  • No student information: the platform asks people not to enter or upload it.
