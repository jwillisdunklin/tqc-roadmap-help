Years 2 and 3: two paths

Core Learning Path

The eight Years 2 and 3 ALEs (10 hours), taken any time across both years, plus standalone sessions you choose until the path reaches the Superintendent Leadership Academy’s 33 hours. Standalone sessions are still being designed; the ones listed now are samples.

33 hours

Superintendent Leadership Academy (Saint Louis University)

Apply in Year 2 or 3. A 33-hour cohort academy in partnership with Saint Louis University. ALE content and facilitation are built into the Academy, so it counts for the eight Years 2 and 3 ALEs.
